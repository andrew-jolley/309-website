309 (Sawbridgeworth) Squadron

Welcome to the Royal Air Force Air Cadets in Sawbridgeworth. We provide a wide range of activities for young people aged 12-20, helping them develop leadership, teamwork, and skills for life.

About Us

More than just flying

We are a small but active squadron serving the local area. We can regularly be spotted leading parades in town with our Marching Band.

Qualifications

Our instructors are qualified to teach Aerospace, Leadership, First Aid, and Drone flying. We also lead external qualifications like the Duke of Edinburgh’s Award.

Achievements

We push towards Wing, Region, and National awards. Recently, our own Cadet Warrant Officer received the Dacre Sword, presented annually to the top All-round Cadet in the UK.

Future Careers

Great for those interested in the Armed Forces, but also builds skills for all careers. Former cadets have moved into Engineering, Finance, Medicine, and Broadcasting.
