Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2026

309 (Sawbridgeworth) Squadron of the Royal Air Force Air Cadets ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or use our services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Voluntary Information: Personal details you provide when submitting enquiry forms (such as Name, Email Address, and Phone Number) for the purpose of joining as a cadet, staff member, or committee member.
  • Automated Information: Anonymous usage data collected via cookies and analytics tools (Google Analytics, Firebase) to help us understand website traffic and performance.
  • Authentication Data: If you are a member of staff or a cadet logging into our internal portals, we process your login credentials via Microsoft Entra ID (formerly Azure Active Directory).
  • Device Management Data: If you enroll a device in our management system (Action1), we collect device identifiers and compliance status to ensure security.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and respond to your membership enquiries.
  • Authenticate authorized users (Staff and Cadets) for access to secure internal systems.
  • Analyze website usage to improve our content and user experience.
  • Comply with legal obligations and RAF Air Cadets regulations.

3. Third-Party Services

We utilize trusted third-party service providers to operate our website and services. These providers may process data on our behalf:

Microsoft Entra ID (Azure)

We use Microsoft Entra ID for identity management and authentication for our Staff and Cadet portals. When you log in, Microsoft processes your credentials, IP address, and sign-in activity to verify your identity and secure your account. For logged-in users, Azure logs access times and device details to help detect suspicious activity and ensure the security of our systems. We do not store your passwords directly on this public website.
Microsoft Privacy Statement

Action1 (Device Management)

For staff and cadets accessing Squadron resources on personal or Squadron-issued devices, we use Action1 to manage security. Action1 allows us to enforce security policies (such as requiring a PIN or encryption) and check device compliance. We collect device identifiers (e.g., model, OS version) but we do not access your personal files, photos, call logs, or browsing history on personal devices.
Action1 Privacy Policy

Cookiebot

We use Cookiebot to manage your cookie consent preferences. Cookiebot automatically scans our website for cookies and allows you to choose which categories of cookies you wish to allow. Your consent status is stored anonymously to remember your choices.
Cookiebot Privacy Policy

Formspree

Our enquiry forms are powered by Formspree. When you submit a form, the data is transmitted securely to Formspree and then emailed to our authorized staff. Formspree may temporarily store submissions for delivery purposes.
Formspree Privacy Policy

Google Analytics & Firebase

We use Google Analytics and Firebase to track visitor statistics. These services use cookies to collect anonymous data about your visit (e.g., pages visited, time spent on site).
Google Privacy Policy

4. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience and for analytics. We use Cookiebot to allow you to manage your consent preferences easily. You can change your choices at any time via the Cookiebot widget or your browser settings.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data. Our website uses HTTPS encryption to ensure data transmitted between your browser and our servers is secure. Authentication services provided by Microsoft Azure utilize enterprise-grade security standards.

6. Your Rights

Under the UK Data Protection Act 2018 and GDPR, you have rights regarding your personal data, including:

  • The right to access the personal data we hold about you.
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate data.
  • The right to request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • The right to withdraw consent for data processing.

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details below.
